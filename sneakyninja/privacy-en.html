Sneaky Ninja Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Sneaky Ninja: Scroll Heist (the “Game”) is made by JustTock, an independent developer (the “Developer”). This policy explains what information the Game handles and what leaves your phone. If there is any difference between translations, the Korean version prevails.

1. At a glance

  • The Game has no account sign-up of its own. It never asks for your email or phone number.
  • The online leaderboard uses Google Play Games. When you post a record, the name you type, your score and the round you reached are stored by Google Play Games and may be visible to other players.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. Device information, the advertising ID, approximate location and similar data may be sent to Google for advertising.
  • The Developer runs no game servers. When the Game hits an error, error and diagnostic information is sent to the Developer through Sentry so the problem can be fixed. It contains no information that identifies you.

2. Information kept only on your phone

3. Information that may leave your phone

Online leaderboard (Google Play Games Services)

The Game provides an online leaderboard through Google Play Games Services.

  • When the Game starts, it automatically tries to sign in to Google Play Games, and Google shares your Play Games profile (nickname, player ID, etc.) with the Game.
  • When you tap “Submit” at the end of a game, your score, together with the name you typed and the round you reached, is posted to the Google Play Games leaderboard. Leaderboard records may be visible to other players, so do not use your real name or other personal information as your name.
  • Whether your entry appears on the leaderboard depends on your Play Games profile visibility settings.

See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles this information. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and game data in the Google Play Games settings.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. To load and show ads, the Google AdMob SDK may send the following to Google:

  • Device information (model, OS version, language, etc.) and the advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions in the app, such as views and clicks
  • Error and diagnostic information from the ad SDK

This information is used to deliver ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud, and Google may show personalized ads based on the advertising ID and app usage. The Developer does not receive or store this information.

In regions that require consent, such as the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, Google’s consent form appears when the Game starts, and you can choose whether to allow personalized ads.

If you do not want personalized ads, you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone’s Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads. For details, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Error and crash reports (Sentry)

When the Game hits an error or crashes, it sends the diagnostic information below to the error tracking service Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., USA) so the problem can be found and fixed.

  • The error message, where in the code it happened, and the game's run log
  • Device model, OS version, device specs (screen, memory, etc.) and app version
  • A record of in-app actions just before the error (such as screen changes)

Information that identifies you, such as your name, email or advertising ID, and the name you enter for the leaderboard are not sent, and storing IP addresses is turned off. The information is stored on Sentry's servers (USA) and deleted automatically after 30 days. See the Sentry Privacy Policy for details.

Update check

When the Game starts, it checks whether a new version is available.

  • It asks the Google Play Store on your phone whether a new version exists.
  • It downloads a small file from the Developer’s website (GitHub Pages) that lists the minimum required version. As with any web request, your IP address and similar data may be logged by GitHub’s servers.

No information is sent to the Developer for the update check.

4. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
